### About

Product engineer, DevOps engineer, UX/UI developer, and **open-source advocate** based in **Tirana, Albania**. I build fast, accessible web products, ship privacy-focused infrastructure, and help OSS teams turn complex systems into clear experiences.

Currently **CTO @ [American Labs](https://american.al)** (product & AI engineering), **UX/UI Developer @ [Ura Design](https://ura.design)**, and running client work through **[ueb.al](https://ueb.al)**. Former **Fedora Ambassador**, ex-member of **The Document Foundation**, and longtime contributor to **Open Source Design**, **GlobaLeaks**, **LibreOffice**, **OpenStreetMap**, and more.

Also find me at **[anxhelo.al](https://anxhelo.al)** and **[angelo.al](https://angelo.al)**.
